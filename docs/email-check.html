Questions

How can I tell if an email is legit?

Check who it is really from (the address, not the name), where replies go, and whether the links open the address they show. Be wary of pressure: a deadline, a locked account, a request for a password or a payment. This checker looks at all of that for you, and at the sender checks (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) when you paste the original message.

What is hidden text in an email?

Text that is in the message but not on your screen: white on white, a font size of zero, moved off the page, or inside an HTML comment. Spammers use it to fool filters. Now it is also used to give instructions to AI agents that read your mail, which see all of it.

Can an AI agent be tricked by an email?

Yes. An agent reads the whole message, hidden parts included, and may follow instructions written there, such as forwarding mail or deleting a message. This is called prompt injection. GigaMail hands your agent only the text you would see, flags the rest, and asks you before anything is sent or deleted.

Is my email uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs in your browser. The message is never sent to GigaMail or anyone else, and remote images in it are blocked, so the sender cannot tell you opened it here.

How do I get the original message?

Easiest: save the mail as a file and open it here. In Gmail, the three dots menu, Download message; in Outlook, drag the mail to your desktop or use Save as. To copy it instead: Gmail, the three dots menu, Show original, Copy to clipboard; Outlook on the web, the three dots menu, View, View message source; Apple Mail, View, Message, Raw Source. Pasting just the text works too, but text copied from the screen leaves out the hidden part and the sender checks.
