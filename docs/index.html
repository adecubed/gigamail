What your agent can do

More than a send button.

Reading, searching, remembering and drafting happen without asking. Only the actions that leave a trace outside wait for you.

Finds anything

"The quote we sent in March" is one question. A local archive keeps every mail and attachment, even after the server drops it.

Reads attachments

PDFs and office files are text to the agent, so the answer comes from the brochure, not from a guess.

Drafts from your documents

Price lists, terms, product sheets. The reply quotes your figures and stops where your documents stop.

Books meetings

Only free slots, inside your office hours. Events are created after you approve. Microsoft 365 or Google Calendar.

Remembers people

History, tone and your own notes sit next to each sender. Every draft to that person reads them first.

Automations

"Mail from these senders gets a draft from these documents." Semi-auto asks you each time; auto runs inside a scope you signed, with an expiry and a daily cap.

All your mailboxes

Microsoft 365 and IMAP: Outlook, Gmail, Aruba, Libero, any server. Several accounts, one view in the console, and each reply leaves from the right address.

Hides what is sensitive

In the desktop app, the masker replaces tax codes, VAT numbers, IBANs and phone numbers before a mail goes to the agent for a draft. Deterministic, no AI. For MCP agents it is planned.

The desktop console adds a mail client built around the agent: ask your mail a question in plain language, dictate a command, see the requests waiting for you, manage automations, and read several accounts as one list. The same engine, without a terminal.

Automations, only if you sign them

The one way a mail leaves without a click: a rule you create in the app, for senders and documents you choose. Semi-auto still asks each time; auto runs inside the scope you signed, with an expiry and a daily cap.

No MCP tool can create or change a rule, so a mail cannot switch on autopilot.

Your data stays local

Index, archive, credentials, memory and configuration live on your machine. GigaMail runs no servers and receives nothing.

What the agent reads is handled by that agent, under its provider's policy.

Sensitive fields hidden

The masker replaces tax codes, VAT numbers, IBANs and phone numbers before a mail goes to the agent for a draft, and puts them back in the draft. Deterministic, no AI.

Today in the desktop app. The same filter for MCP agents is planned.

An email is input, not authority. Message content is treated as untrusted data. Credentials never pass through the agent channel, so an instruction hidden in a mail cannot add an account or read a secret.

Questions

Agentic email, in plain words.

What is agentic email?

Email where an AI agent does the reading, searching and drafting for you, and a person keeps the decisions that matter. In GigaMail the agent prepares; sending, replying and deleting wait for your approval.

Which AI agents work with GigaMail?

Claude Code and Claude Desktop, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes and any client that speaks MCP. GigaMail has no AI of its own: the intelligence is the agent you connect, and its data policy is the one that applies to what it reads.

Which mailboxes can I connect?

Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com through Microsoft Graph, and any IMAP server: Gmail, Aruba, Libero and others. Several accounts side by side, each replying from its own address.

Where is my data?

On your machine. Mail index, archive, credentials, memory and configuration stay local; GigaMail runs no servers and receives nothing. In the desktop app, the masker can hide sensitive fields before the agent drafts a reply. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Can anything be sent without my approval?

Only through an automation rule you created in the app and signed, inside its scope, expiry and daily cap. No MCP tool can create or change a rule, so an agent or a mail cannot switch one on.

What does GigaMail cost?

Nothing. The desktop app and the MCP server are free: no account, no subscription, no ads. GigaMail is open source under the AGPL-3.0, on GitHub and PyPI.

How is GigaMail different from AgentMail?

AgentMail creates new inboxes for AI agents in its own cloud, driven by an API. GigaMail connects your agent to the mailbox you already use, runs on your computer, and keeps every send, reply and delete behind your approval. See the side-by-side comparison.

How is GigaMail different from Composio?

Composio gives an agent tools for over a thousand apps through its cloud, which keeps the logins and makes the calls. GigaMail does only mail and calendar, from your computer, with every send, reply and delete behind your approval. The two can work side by side. See the comparison.

How is GigaMail different from Copilot in Outlook?

Copilot is Microsoft's assistant inside Outlook: a paid add-on to Microsoft 365 (from $21 per user per month on top of the base licence), with the model Microsoft chooses, running in Microsoft's cloud and working on Microsoft mailboxes. GigaMail is free, runs on your machine, works with Microsoft 365 and any IMAP mailbox, and lets you choose the agent: Claude, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes or any MCP client. And the agent cannot approve its own actions: sending and deleting wait for you, confirmed outside its reach. See the comparison.
