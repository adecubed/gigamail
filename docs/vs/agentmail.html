GigaMail vs AgentMail

Looking for an AgentMail alternative? Both give AI agents email, in two different ways. AgentMail is a hosted API that creates new inboxes for your agents. GigaMail is an open-source MCP server that connects your agent to the mailboxes you already have, keeps everything on your machine, and lets nothing be sent or deleted until you approve it.

The short answer

Choose AgentMail

if you are building a product where each agent needs its own email address, created by API, at scale.

Choose GigaMail

if you want Claude, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes or any MCP client to read, search and draft replies in your own Outlook, Gmail or IMAP mailbox, with you approving every send.

When each one fits

AgentMail is the better fit when:

  • You ship agents to customers and need to provision many inboxes programmatically.
  • Agents must sign up for services or receive verification codes under their own address.
  • You want a managed service, with dedicated IPs and a SOC 2 report on the Startup plan.

GigaMail is the better fit when:

  • You want your agent on the email you already get: search, triage, replies drafted from your price lists and documents, appointments in your calendar.
  • You do not want your mail, credentials or index on someone else's server.
  • You want a human in the loop that the agent cannot bypass: it gets an inert request id, you approve with Hello or Touch ID.
  • You want something free and open source you can read, audit and modify.

FAQ

Is GigaMail an open-source AgentMail alternative?

For agents that work on your own mailboxes, yes: GigaMail is AGPL-3.0 and free. If each agent needs its own new address created by API, that is what AgentMail does and GigaMail does not.

Can my agent send or delete email without me?

No. Send, reply, move and delete return only an inert request id. They run after you approve with Windows Hello or Touch ID (a local PIN on Linux), with the exact arguments you saw. An agent with a shell can open that prompt but cannot pass it.

Where is my email stored?

On your machine: archive, search index, credentials, sender memory and configuration. GigaMail runs no service and receives nothing. The mail your agent reads goes to that agent's model provider, so choose your agent accordingly; the masker can hide tax codes, IBANs, emails and phone numbers first.

Which mailboxes and agents does it work with?

Microsoft 365 and Outlook through Microsoft Graph, and any IMAP account, Gmail included. Plugins for Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw and Hermes; any other MCP client through gigamail-server.
