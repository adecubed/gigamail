Comparison

GigaMail and Composio work at different layers.

Composio connects an agent to over a thousand apps through its cloud: it keeps the logins and makes the calls. GigaMail does one job, your mail and calendar, from your computer, and keeps sending, replying and deleting behind your approval.

Which one fits

Pick Composio if

  • your agent needs many apps at once (Slack, GitHub, a CRM…) through one SDK or one hosted MCP endpoint;
  • you build a product where each of your users connects their own accounts, and you want the OAuth handled for you;
  • a hosted service that stores the tokens and runs the calls is fine (self-hosting is an Enterprise arrangement).

Pick GigaMail if

  • mail is the job: Microsoft 365, Outlook, Gmail or any IMAP server, with attachments, your documents and the calendar;
  • mail, index and credentials should stay on your computer;
  • nothing should leave until a person approves it, outside the agent’s reach.

Using both

They do not exclude each other. An agent can use Composio for Slack, GitHub or a CRM, and GigaMail for the mail, so that sending, replying and deleting stay on your computer and behind your approval.
