Comparison

GigaMail and Copilot bring AI to your mail in different ways.

Copilot is Microsoft’s assistant built into Outlook: Microsoft’s model, in Microsoft’s cloud, on Microsoft mailboxes. GigaMail connects the agent you choose to your mail and calendar from your computer, on Microsoft 365 or any IMAP mailbox, and keeps sending and deleting behind your approval.

Which one fits

Pick Copilot if

  • your company runs on Microsoft 365 with Exchange Online, and IT wants the assistant licensed, managed and covered by Microsoft’s compliance like the rest;
  • you want it inside the Outlook apps you already use, phone included, and in Word, Teams and the rest of Microsoft 365;
  • Microsoft’s cloud and the model Microsoft picks are fine for your mail.

Pick GigaMail if

  • your mail is not only on Exchange Online: Outlook.com, Gmail or any IMAP server (Aruba, Libero…), with attachments, your documents and the calendar;
  • you want to choose the agent: Claude, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, a local model or any MCP client;
  • it should cost nothing, keep mail, index and credentials on your computer, and send nothing until a person approves it, outside the agent’s reach.

Using both

They do not exclude each other. GigaMail works with Microsoft 365 mailboxes through Microsoft Graph, so on the same mailbox you can keep Copilot in Outlook and also use Claude, Codex or another agent through GigaMail, with sending and deleting behind your approval. And GigaMail covers the IMAP mailboxes Copilot does not reach.
