Comparison

GigaMail and AgentMail solve different problems.

AgentMail gives an AI agent its own new inbox, hosted in its cloud and driven by an API. GigaMail connects an agent to the mailbox you already use, from your computer, and keeps sending, replying and deleting behind your approval.

Which one fits

Pick AgentMail if

  • your agent needs addresses of its own, created by code, on agentmail.to or your domain;
  • you build a product that sends and receives at scale through an API, with webhooks;
  • a hosted service in the United States (or the EU on Enterprise) is fine for that mail.

Pick GigaMail if

  • the agent should work on your mail: Microsoft 365, Outlook, Gmail or any IMAP server;
  • mail, index and credentials should stay on your computer;
  • nothing should leave until a person approves it, outside the agent’s reach.
