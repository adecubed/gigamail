# GigaMail with ChatGPT: setup in 5 minutes

GigaMail connects your mailbox (Microsoft 365, Outlook.com, or any IMAP
provider such as Network Solutions) to ChatGPT. Ask ChatGPT to search,
read and summarize your mail and to draft replies. Nothing is sent until
you approve it in GigaMail.

## 1. Install GigaMail and add your mailbox

1. Download GigaMail from [gigamail.ai](https://gigamail.ai) and install it
   (Windows 10/11 or macOS, no administrator password).
2. Open it and add your mailbox: **Microsoft** (sign in in the browser) or
   **IMAP** (address, password, and the IMAP/SMTP servers from your
   provider's help page).

Your password stays on your computer, encrypted. ChatGPT never sees it.

## 2. Use it from ChatGPT

1. Install the **ChatGPT desktop app** and sign in.
2. In the app, switch to **Codex**.
3. Open the **Plugins** directory and install **GigaMail**.
4. In GigaMail, click **Connect** under *ChatGPT and Codex* (in the
   first-run guide, or in **Automations**). Then restart the ChatGPT app.
5. In a new chat, ask: *List my GigaMail accounts.*

## 3. Or use it from GigaMail (beta)

In GigaMail, open **Automations** and choose **Codex** as the agent. Then
ask directly in the bar at the bottom of the window, for example:
*Summarize the last mail from the bank.*

## Approving

Sending, replying, deleting and calendar changes wait for you under
**Waiting for you** in GigaMail. Review, then **Approve** or **Reject**.

## Privacy

The text of a mail you ask about is sent to OpenAI to produce the answer,
under your ChatGPT plan's terms. Your password never is.
